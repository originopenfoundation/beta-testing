Dependency Impact Simulation™

Governance Architecture Space

Dependency Impact Simulation™ governs the architectural space
responsible for evaluating, modeling, and understanding how
governance decisions, operational changes, implementations,
failures, or interventions may affect dependency structures across
complex governance environments.

The space exists because modern systems are interconnected.

A change affecting one component frequently affects many others.

Organizations often understand individual systems while
underestimating the influence of dependency relationships.

Dependency Impact Simulation™ exists to reveal these effects before
operational reality reveals them through consequence.

Canonical Definition

Dependency Impact Simulation™ is the governable architectural space
responsible for evaluating, modeling, and understanding the effects
that governance actions, operational changes, risks, failures, or
interventions may have on dependency structures within complex
governance environments.

The Core Problem

Organizations frequently evaluate direct outcomes while overlooking
dependency consequences.

As a result:

  • hidden impacts emerge unexpectedly,
  • implementation side effects increase,
  • critical dependencies become destabilized,
  • governance interventions create secondary problems,
  • operational resilience decreases.


Many governance failures originate not from the original action but
from its dependency consequences.

Dependency Impact Simulation™ was created to address this challenge.

Why Dependency Impact Simulation™ Exists

Every governance environment contains dependency structures.

Dependencies may connect:

  • systems,
  • organizations,
  • processes,
  • authority structures,
  • operational functions,
  • information pathways,
  • value flows.


A change affecting one dependency may influence many others.

Dependency Impact Simulation™ exists to evaluate these effects
before implementation occurs.

The Dependency Principle

A fundamental principle of Dependency Impact Simulation™ is:

Every governance dependency creates the possibility of
indirect impact.

The objective is not only understanding what changes.

The objective is understanding what else changes because of it.

Core Questions

Dependency Impact Simulation™ seeks to answer:

Which dependencies may be affected?

Which dependencies are critical?

Which dependencies may become unstable?

Which dependencies may create secondary consequences?

Which dependencies may amplify risk?

Which dependencies may increase resilience?

Which dependencies may create implementation barriers?

Which dependencies may become future failure points?

Types of Dependency Impact

Operational Dependency Impact

Effects on operational continuity and execution.

Governance Dependency Impact

Effects on governance structures and governance effectiveness.

Authority Dependency Impact

Effects on authority relationships, accountability, and
responsibility structures.

Information Dependency Impact

Effects on information flows and decision support systems.

Value Dependency Impact

Effects on value creation, transfer, preservation, concentration,
and loss.

Autonomous System Dependency Impact

Effects on AI systems, autonomous agents, robotics environments, and
self-healing systems.

Operational Reality Dependency Impact

Effects emerging through real-world implementation conditions.

Dependency Impact Simulation™ vs Dependency Discovery™

The two spaces perform different functions.

Dependency Discovery™

Answers:

  • What dependencies exist?


Dependency Impact Simulation™

Answers:

  • What happens if those dependencies change?


Discovery identifies structures.

Simulation evaluates behavior.

Dependency Impact Simulation™ vs Risk Simulation™

The two spaces are complementary.

Risk Simulation™

Evaluates governance risks.

Dependency Impact Simulation™

Evaluates dependency behavior and dependency consequences.

Risk Simulation™ focuses on threats.

Dependency Impact Simulation™ focuses on interconnected effects.

Position Within Governance Simulation™

Dependency Impact Simulation™ follows:

  • Scenario Modeling™
  • Future State Modeling™
  • Risk Simulation™


Once future conditions and risks have been identified, Dependency
Impact Simulation™ evaluates how those conditions may influence
dependency structures throughout the governance environment.

Relationship to Governance Architecture On Demand™

Dependency findings support architecture generation.

Effective governance architectures must account not only for direct
outcomes but also for dependency behavior.

Dependency Impact Simulation™ helps determine:

  • architecture resilience,
  • governance controls,
  • implementation safeguards,
  • dependency management requirements.


Relationship to Governance Intelligence Analytics™

Governance Intelligence Analytics™ utilizes Dependency Impact
Simulation™ to understand the indirect consequences of governance
decisions before recommendations are generated.

Simulation findings contribute directly to:

  • Governance Intelligence Reports™,
  • Governance Diagnostics™,
  • Governance Simulations™,
  • Architecture Recommendations™.


Why This Space Matters

As governance environments become increasingly interconnected,
indirect consequences become increasingly important.

Organizations often discover dependency effects only after
implementation occurs.

Dependency Impact Simulation™ exists to identify and understand
these effects before operational reality exposes them.

Canonical Closing Statement

Dependency Impact Simulation™ transforms dependency structures into
governance intelligence. By evaluating how governance actions,
operational changes, risks, and interventions affect interconnected
systems, it enables

organizations, AI systems, autonomous agents, and future governance
environments to understand indirect consequences before operational
reality reveals them.
