What Is Responsibility Mapping Analysis™?

Responsibility Mapping Analysis™ is a professional
assessment and intelligence service focused on identifying,
tracing, documenting, validating, and analyzing responsibility
relationships across organizational, operational, contractual,
governance, AI, and multi-party environments.

Many organizations experience recurring conflicts because
responsibility is assumed rather than mapped.

As a result:
  • responsibility becomes fragmented
  • responsibility becomes transferred informally
  • responsibility gaps emerge
  • operational failures occur
  • accountability becomes unclear
Responsibility Mapping Analysis3™ exists to identify responsibility
before responsibility becomes disputed.

Why This Service Exists

Organizations frequently ask:

Who should do the work?

However, the more important question is often:

Who is actually responsible?

In complex environments involving:
  • contractors
  • subcontractors
  • staffing agencies
  • AI systems
  • autonomous systems
  • external providers
  • multiple departments
responsibility often becomes difficult to identify.

Responsibility Mapping Analysis™ was created to address
this challenge.
