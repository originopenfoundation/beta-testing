Canonical Definition

Responsibility Continuity Origin Integrity Module (RCOIM) defines
the structural conditions under which responsibility-continuity
relationships remain attributable to identifiable, legitimate,
traceable, governable, reconstructable, accountable, and
operationally valid responsibility sources throughout
the responsibility lifecycle.

RCOIM governs responsibility-continuity origin.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine where responsibility continuity originated, which
responsibility relationship is being preserved, and whether the
continuity relationship remains connected to a
legitimate responsibility source.

Module Operational Role

RCOIM defines the continuity-origin governance layer of RCS.

Its role is to preserve visibility into the original responsibility
relationship from which continuity emerges.

Module Operational Space

RCOIM governs:

  • continuity origin
  • responsibility-source continuity
  • continuity attribution
  • continuity provenance
  • preserved responsibility origin
  • continuity legitimacy
  • responsibility-preservation origin
  • continuity governance


The module applies wherever governance requires reconstruction
of continuity origins.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • identifiable continuity sources
  • traceable continuity origins
  • reconstructable continuity history
  • accountable continuity relationships
  • governance-valid continuity attribution
  • operationally valid continuity provenance


Its function is to ensure that governance can determine which
original responsibility relationship remains preserved
throughout operational reality.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Continuity Origin Object

The organization must define which continuity environments
require origin governance.

This may include:

  • organizational structures
  • management systems
  • contractor environments
  • subcontractor chains
  • project environments
  • AI governance systems
  • Human-AI operational environments
  • governance frameworks


2. Define Continuity Origin Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which continuity
origin remains valid.

This includes:

  • continuity-source requirements
  • attribution requirements
  • legitimacy requirements
  • preservation requirements
  • governance requirements
  • continuity-origin conditions


3. Define Origin Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how continuity-origin failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • unidentified responsibility origins
  • undocumented continuity relationships
  • illegitimate continuity structures
  • governance-blind responsibility preservation
  • continuity-source ambiguity
  • unverifiable continuity origins


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
continuity-origin failures.

Governance response may include:

  • continuity review
  • source verification
  • governance intervention
  • continuity reconstruction
  • corrective actions
  • continuity reassessment
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • responsibility origins
  • continuity relationships
  • governance reviews
  • validation activities
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting continuity states


A responsibility-continuity environment must not remain origin-valid
if materially significant continuity relationships cannot be
attributed, reconstructed, reviewed, validated, or governed.

Use Case 1 — Contractor Handover Environment

Scenario

Responsibility passes through multiple contractors and supervisors
before an operational outcome occurs.

Application

RCOIM reconstructs the original responsibility relationship and
verifies whether continuity remained connected to that origin.

Result

Governance gains visibility into the source and legitimacy
of responsibility continuity.

Use Case 2 — Human-AI Operational

Environment

Scenario

Responsibilities move between human supervisors, orchestration
systems, and autonomous agents throughout execution.

Application

RCOIM reconstructs the original responsibility relationship and
verifies continuity preservation across intelligent
operational environments.

Result

Governance gains visibility into continuity origins across
Human-AI ecosystems.
