Methodology

The platform separates three layers: Verified Fact, OOF® Classification, and OOF® Governance Assessment. OOF® analysis is never presented as an externally established fact.

Source Collection and Verification

Each factual field retains source provenance. Public records require review status and evidence confidence. Copyrighted articles are not reproduced.

Incident Monitoring and Assessment

Monitoring is broad and lightweight. Full architecture assessment and stress testing are selective, evidence-based, versioned, and subject to human review.

Architecture Mapping

Architecture identities come from the official OOF® Architecture Index. Automated relevance does not constitute an approved Architecture Assessment and never creates or modifies OOF® methodology.

Coverage States

Public monitoring distinguishes Architecture Identified, Architecture Review Required, No OOF® Architecture Identified, and Not Assessed. A missing automated match is not a confirmed governance gap.

Dataset Scope

Counts and rates describe only the approved public dataset and must not be treated as absolute proof of architecture quality, company fault, legal liability, regulatory non-compliance, or global incident prevalence.
