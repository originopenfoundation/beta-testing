What Is Problem Space Mapping™?

Problem Space Mapping™ is a professional assessment
and intelligence service focused on identifying, classifying,
mapping, and understanding the actual problem space before
solutions, technologies, methodologies, organizational changes,
or governance interventions are proposed.

Many organizations attempt to solve problems immediately.

However, organizations often discover that:
  • the wrong problem was analyzed
  • symptoms were mistaken for causes
  • responsibility was assigned incorrectly
  • governance gaps were overlooked
  • dependencies remained hidden
Problem Space Mapping™ exists to identify the real problem
before resources are committed to solving it.

Why This Service Exists

Organizations routinely invest significant resources into:
  • consultants
  • technologies
