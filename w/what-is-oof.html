What is OOF®?

OOF® — OriginOpen® Foundation

OOF® (OriginOpen® Foundation) is the registered trademark and
official abbreviation of OriginOpen® Foundation.

OOF® is an independent Methodological Reference Authority
dedicated to developing structured methodologies, governance
architectures, standards, operational frameworks, methodology
infrastructure, and Structured Reality™ Standards for organizations,
artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, and future digital
ecosystems.

OOF® develops Structured Reality™ Standards to define the
structural conditions under which systems, methodologies, AI outputs,
governance processes, and operational decisions can be considered
valid, interoperable, explainable, and aligned with reality.

OOF® does not build software.

OOF® does not operate AI systems.

OOF® develops the methodologies and Structured Reality™ Standards
that make trustworthy systems possible.
