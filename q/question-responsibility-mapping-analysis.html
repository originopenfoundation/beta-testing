Responsibility Mapping Analysis™ Question Index

OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™)

Mapping Responsibility, Accountability, Authority, Delegation, and
Ownership Relationships

Organizations frequently experience failures not because work was
not performed, but because responsibility was unclear.

People assume others are responsible.

Authority exists without accountability.

Accountability exists without authority.

Responsibilities are delegated without oversight.

Ownership remains undefined.

Responsibility Mapping Analysis™ exists to identify, analyze,
document, and understand responsibility relationships across
organizations, governance systems, supply chains, projects, AI
systems, autonomous agents, contractors, and
operational environments.

This Question Index™ provides a structured collection of questions
designed to support responsibility analysis, accountability mapping,
authority assessment, delegation review, and
ownership clarification.

The objective is not merely to assign responsibility.

The objective is to understand how responsibility operates.

About the OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™) is a growing
collection of structured questions designed to help identify,
understand, classify, and investigate problems before searching
for solutions.

Questions are organized into categories, clusters, and problem
environments to improve clarity, analysis, governance,
and decision-making.

The objective is not merely to provide answers.

The objective is to ensure that the right questions are being asked.
