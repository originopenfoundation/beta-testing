Methodology Existence Validation™ Question Index

OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™)

Determining Whether a True Methodology Exists and Can Be
Demonstrated, Documented, and Validated

Organizations frequently claim to possess methodologies.

Consultants claim methodologies.

Companies claim methodologies.

Projects claim methodologies.

Experts claim methodologies.

However, many claimed methodologies are simply:

  • experiences,
  • opinions,
  • undocumented practices,
  • isolated processes,
  • collections of documents,
  • informal know-how.


Methodology Existence Validation™ exists to determine whether a true
methodology actually exists.

This Question Index™ provides a structured collection of questions
designed to support methodology existence verification,
documentation assessment, structural analysis, and
evidence-based validation.

The objective is not to assume a methodology exists.

The objective is to determine whether its existence can
be demonstrated.

Methodology Identification

Does a methodology actually exist? Is the methodology clearly
identifiable? Can the methodology be distinguished from
general knowledge?

Can the methodology be distinguished from a process? Can the
methodology be distinguished from a framework? Can the methodology
be distinguished from personal expertise? Is the methodology
explicitly defined? Does the methodology have a clear purpose? Is
the methodology consistently described? Can the methodology be
independently recognized?

Methodology Documentation

Is the methodology documented? Is documentation complete? Is
documentation accessible? Can documentation be reviewed? Are
methodology components documented? Are methodology principles
documented? Are methodology boundaries documented? Is version
history available? Is documentation maintained? Can documentation
support validation?

Methodology Structure

Does the methodology possess structure? Are methodology components
identifiable? Are methodology relationships defined? Are operational
conditions specified? Are governance conditions specified? Are
methodology rules defined? Can the methodology be reproduced? Can
the methodology be explained? Is methodology architecture visible?
Does structure remain consistent?

Methodology Evidence

What evidence demonstrates existence? Can methodology outputs be
observed? Can methodology implementation be demonstrated? Are
operational records available? Are historical records available? Can
methodology use be verified? Is evidence independent? Is evidence
traceable? Can evidence be audited? Is evidence sufficient to
support existence claims?

Methodology Repeatability

Can the methodology be repeated? Can different users apply it
consistently? Do similar inputs produce similar outcomes? Can
operational procedures be reproduced? Are methodology conditions
stable? Can repeatability be measured?

Is repeatability documented? Are repeatability limitations known?
Does repeatability depend on individuals? Can repeatability be
independently verified?

Methodology Validation

Has the methodology been validated? Are validation criteria defined?
Can validation results be demonstrated? Is validation evidence
available? Has validation been independently reviewed? Can
validation be reproduced? Are validation limitations known? Are
validation assumptions documented? Does validation support existence
claims? Is ongoing validation required?

Methodology Independence

Does the methodology exist independently of individuals? Does the
methodology survive personnel changes? Can it be transferred to
others? Can it be taught? Can it be documented without the original
creator? Does it rely upon undocumented knowledge? Is organizational
dependence present? Can the methodology operate across environments?
Does the methodology remain identifiable over time? Is
independence demonstrable?

Methodology Governance

Who governs the methodology? Is governance documented? Are
methodology changes controlled? Is version control maintained? Are
responsibilities defined? Is accountability assigned? Can governance
decisions be audited? Does governance support continuity? Are
governance risks known? Is governance maturity sufficient?

Methodology Authenticity

Is the methodology authentic? Can originality be demonstrated? Are
methodology claims supported by evidence? Is the methodology
genuinely operational? Are marketing claims distinguishable from
reality? Can authenticity be independently assessed? Does evidence
support authenticity? Are authenticity risks known? Is authenticity
maintained over time?

Can authenticity be validated?

Methodology Continuity

Has the methodology evolved over time? Is continuity preserved? Are
historical versions available? Can evolution be traced? Is
methodology knowledge preserved? Can future development be managed?
Is succession planning possible? Does continuity support long-term
existence? Can methodology identity survive change? Is long-term
continuity demonstrable?

Related OOF® Service

Methodology Existence Validation™ helps organizations determine

About the OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™) is a growing
collection of structured questions designed to help identify,
understand, classify, and investigate problems before searching
for solutions.

Questions are organized into categories, clusters, and problem
environments to improve clarity, analysis, governance,
and decision-making.

The objective is not merely to provide answers.

The objective is to ensure that the right questions are being asked.
