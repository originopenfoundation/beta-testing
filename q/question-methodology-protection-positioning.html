Methodology Protection & Positioning™ Question Index

OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™)

Protecting, Differentiating, Positioning, and Strengthening
Methodology Assets

Many organizations invest significant time, expertise, resources,
and knowledge into developing methodologies.

However, valuable methodologies often remain:

  • unprotected,
  • poorly positioned,
  • difficult to differentiate,
  • difficult to identify,
  • difficult to communicate,
  • difficult to commercialize.


Methodology Protection & Positioning™ exists to help organizations
strengthen methodology assets through strategic protection,
differentiation, positioning, classification, and long-term
value preservation.

This Question Index™ provides a structured collection of questions
designed to support methodology protection, positioning, ownership
clarity, strategic differentiation, and market readiness.

The objective is not merely to protect methodologies.

The objective is to ensure methodologies remain identifiable,
defensible, valuable, and strategically positioned over time.

Methodology Identity

What is the methodology called? Is the methodology name distinctive?
Is the methodology easily identifiable? Does the methodology have a
clear identity? Is the methodology consistently described? Can the
methodology be distinguished from alternatives? Does the methodology
require renaming? Is the methodology identity stable? Does the
methodology identity support growth? Can the methodology be
recognized independently?

Methodology Ownership

Who owns the methodology? Is ownership clearly documented? Can
ownership be demonstrated? Are ownership rights protected? Are
ownership claims disputed? Is ownership continuity preserved? Can
ownership transfer be managed? Are contributors identified? Is
intellectual ownership clear? Are ownership risks understood?

Methodology Differentiation

What makes the methodology unique? How does it differ from
alternatives? Can differentiation be demonstrated? Are unique
characteristics documented? Does the methodology solve problems
differently? Can competitors easily replicate it? Are
differentiators sustainable? Does differentiation create value? Is
differentiation clearly communicated? Does differentiation support
market positioning?

Methodology Protection

Can the methodology be protected? Should trademark protection be
considered? Should methodology registration be considered? Are
protection mechanisms documented? Are intellectual assets
identified? Can future developments be protected? Are protection
gaps present? Is methodology continuity preserved? Can protection
strengthen adoption? Is long-term protection realistic?

Methodology Positioning

How should the methodology be positioned? Who is the intended
audience? Which industries should be targeted? Which problems does
the methodology address? Which opportunities does it create? Does
positioning reflect methodology strengths? Is positioning clearly
defined? Can positioning be improved? Does positioning support
growth? Is positioning aligned with strategic objectives?

Methodology Communication

Is the methodology easy to explain? Is the methodology
understandable? Are key messages clearly defined? Can non-experts
understand its value? Are benefits clearly communicated? Are
limitations clearly communicated? Does communication create
confusion? Can communication be simplified? Is messaging consistent?
Does communication support adoption?

Methodology Architecture Placement

Where does the methodology belong? Does it fit within a larger
framework? Does it belong within a governance architecture? Can
architectural relationships be identified? Are dependencies
understood? Can architecture placement improve clarity? Are related
methodologies known? Can architectural positioning increase value?
Is methodology scope clearly defined? Does placement support
long-term evolution?

Methodology Commercial Positioning

Can the methodology support commercial services? Can it support
licensing? Can it support consulting? Can it support training? Can
it support certification? Can it support software solutions? Does
positioning improve commercialization? Is the target market clearly
defined? Are value propositions identified? Is commercial
positioning realistic?

Methodology Reputation

Is the methodology trusted? Can trust be demonstrated? Is evidence
available? Are references available? Can credibility be
strengthened? Does the methodology have a track record? Are
reputation risks known? Can reputation be measured? Does reputation
support adoption? Is trust transferable across environments?

Long-Term Strategic Positioning

Will the methodology remain relevant? Can future versions be
developed? Can new modules be added?

Can future standards emerge from it? Can future architectures evolve
around it? Does positioning support longevity? Can methodology value
increase over time? Is strategic direction clearly defined? Can
future opportunities be identified? What is the long-term
positioning strategy?

About the OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™) is a growing
collection of structured questions designed to help identify,
understand, classify, and investigate problems before searching
for solutions.

Questions are organized into categories, clusters, and problem
environments to improve clarity, analysis, governance,
and decision-making.

The objective is not merely to provide answers.

The objective is to ensure that the right questions are being asked.
