Methodology Valuation™ Question Index

OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™)

Understanding the Value of Methodologies Before Licensing,
Acquiring, Commercializing, or Scaling Them

Organizations routinely value:

  • companies,
  • intellectual property,
  • patents,
  • brands,
  • products,
  • technologies.


Far fewer assess the value of methodologies themselves.

Methodology Valuation™ exists to help organizations understand the
strategic, operational, intellectual, governance, and economic value
of methodological assets.

This Question Index™ provides a structured collection of questions
designed to support methodology valuation, opportunity assessment,
licensing decisions, commercialization analysis, and
strategic evaluation.

The objective is not to immediately determine value.

The objective is to understand the factors that create value.

Methodology Value Creation

What value does the methodology create? What problem does the
methodology solve? How significant is the problem being solved? Does
the methodology create measurable outcomes? Can value creation be
demonstrated? Is value creation repeatable? Does the methodology
improve performance? Does the methodology reduce risk? Does the
methodology increase efficiency? Does the methodology create
competitive advantage?

Methodology Differentiation

What makes the methodology different? Is the methodology unique? Can
the methodology be easily replicated? Does the methodology offer a
novel approach? Does the methodology solve problems differently? Are
differentiators clearly defined? Can differentiation be
demonstrated? Is differentiation sustainable? Does the methodology
create barriers to imitation? Does differentiation increase value?

Methodology Market Demand

Is there market demand for the methodology? Which industries may
benefit most? How large is the potential market? Is demand growing
or declining? Are organizations actively seeking similar solutions?
Does the methodology address emerging needs? Can demand be
demonstrated? Are potential users identifiable? Is adoption likely?
Does the methodology solve a recognized problem?

Methodology Commercialization

Can the methodology be licensed? Can the methodology be sold as a
service? Can it support consulting services? Can it support training
programs? Can it support certification programs? Can it support
software products? Can it support governance frameworks? Can it
support recurring revenue? Are commercialization pathways defined?
Is commercialization practical?

Methodology Scalability

Can the methodology scale globally? Can it be adapted across
industries? Can it support large organizations? Can it support small
organizations? Can it operate across jurisdictions? Can it support
multiple use cases? Does scalability increase value? Are scalability
constraints known? Can growth be sustained? Is expansion realistic?

Methodology Adoption Potential

How easily can organizations adopt the methodology? Does adoption
require specialized expertise? Are implementation requirements
reasonable? Can adoption be accelerated? Are barriers to adoption
known? Can benefits be communicated clearly? Is training required?
Can adoption be measured? Is adoption likely to create network
effects? Does adoption improve long-term value?

Methodology Strategic Value

Does the methodology create strategic advantages? Does it support
long-term organizational goals? Can it improve governance? Can it
improve decision-making? Can it improve operational resilience? Can
it support innovation? Does it create intellectual leverage? Can it
influence industry practices? Does it strengthen organizational
capability? Does it create sustainable advantages?

Methodology Intellectual Value

Does the methodology represent original know-how? Can intellectual
contribution be demonstrated? Does the methodology contain unique
knowledge? Can the methodology be protected? Does intellectual depth
increase value? Is knowledge transferable? Does the methodology
preserve expertise? Can future development increase value? Is
intellectual ownership clear? Does intellectual uniqueness
contribute to valuation?

Methodology Risk Factors

What risks may reduce methodology value? Does value depend on
specific individuals? Can competitors replicate the methodology? Are
governance risks present? Are adoption risks present? Are
scalability risks present? Are commercialization assumptions
realistic? Could value decline over time? Are dependencies clearly
identified? Is value resilient to market changes?

Methodology Future Potential

Can the methodology evolve? Can new modules be added? Can new
standards be derived?

Can future services be built around it? Can future architectures
emerge from it? Does it create new opportunities? Can it support
future technologies? Can it support AI environments? Can it remain
relevant over time? What future value creation pathways exist?

About the OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™) is a growing
collection of structured questions designed to help identify,
understand, classify, and investigate problems before searching
for solutions.

Questions are organized into categories, clusters, and problem
environments to improve clarity, analysis, governance,
and decision-making.

The objective is not merely to provide answers.

The objective is to ensure that the right questions are being asked.
