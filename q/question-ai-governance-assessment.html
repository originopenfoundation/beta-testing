AI Governance Assessment™ Question Index

OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™)

Assessing AI Governance Readiness, Accountability, Explainability,
Oversight, Risk, and Operational Control

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly moving from experimentation into
operational environments.

Organizations increasingly deploy:

  • AI assistants,
  • autonomous agents,
  • decision-support systems,
  • machine learning systems,
  • AI-powered workflows,
  • multi-agent environments.


As AI capabilities grow, governance requirements become
increasingly important.

AI Governance Assessment™ exists to help organizations evaluate
whether AI systems operate within defined governance conditions.

This Question Index™ provides a structured collection of questions
designed to support AI governance assessment, accountability
analysis, oversight evaluation, explainability review, risk
identification, and governance readiness analysis.

The objective is not merely to evaluate AI.

The objective is to understand whether AI can operate responsibly,
transparently, and governably within real-world environments.

AI Governance Foundations

Does an AI governance framework exist? Is AI governance formally
documented? Are AI governance objectives clearly defined? Is
governance applied consistently across AI systems? Are governance
responsibilities assigned? Is governance oversight established? Can
governance effectiveness be measured?

Are governance policies maintained? Is AI governance operational or
theoretical? Is governance sufficient for deployment?

AI Accountability

Who is accountable for AI decisions? Who approves AI deployment? Who
owns AI outcomes? Can accountability be demonstrated? Are
accountability relationships documented? Are accountability gaps
present? Can accountability be audited? Does accountability remain
clear during autonomous operation? Are accountability escalation
paths defined? Is accountability enforceable?

Human Oversight

Does human oversight exist? Who supervises AI operations? Can humans
intervene? Can AI actions be stopped? Are override mechanisms
defined? Are intervention conditions documented? Can oversight
effectiveness be measured? Are oversight responsibilities assigned?
Does oversight remain effective at scale? Is human
control preserved?

AI Explainability

Can AI decisions be explained? Can AI outputs be interpreted? Is
decision logic understandable? Can explanations be reviewed? Are
explanations consistent? Can explanation quality be measured? Are
explanation limitations documented? Can stakeholders understand
outcomes? Does explainability support trust? Is explainability
sufficient for operational use?

AI Decision Governance

What decisions can AI make? Which decisions require human approval?
Are decision boundaries documented? Can AI delegate decisions? Are
decision rights defined? Are decision pathways traceable? Can
decisions be audited? Are high-risk decisions restricted? Can
decision failures be analyzed? Is decision governance effective?

AI Risk Assessment

What risks does the AI system create? Are AI risks documented? Can
AI risks be measured? Are emerging risks monitored? Can AI produce
harmful outcomes? Can AI be manipulated? Are operational risks
understood? Are governance risks understood? Are mitigation
mechanisms established? Is AI risk exposure acceptable?

AI Authority and Permissions

What authority does AI possess? Who granted that authority? Are
authority limits defined? Can authority be modified? Can authority
be revoked? Are permissions documented? Are permission boundaries
enforced? Can authority escalation occur? Are authority conflicts
possible? Is authority governance sufficient?

AI Auditability

Can AI actions be audited? Are activity records maintained? Can
decisions be reconstructed? Are audit trails complete? Is evidence
preserved? Can audits be performed independently? Are audit
requirements documented? Can audit failures be identified? Does
auditability support accountability? Is audit readiness sufficient?

Multi-Agent Governance

Does the AI interact with other agents? Are agent relationships
governed? Can agent authority conflicts occur? Can agent
responsibilities be identified? Are coordination rules defined? Can
multi-agent decisions be traced? Are escalation mechanisms
established? Are agent dependencies documented? Can multi-agent
risks be assessed? Is multi-agent governance sufficient?

Future AI Governance Readiness

Is governance prepared for autonomous agents? Is governance prepared
for self-improving systems? Is governance prepared for large-scale
deployment? Is governance prepared for AI-human collaboration? Is
governance prepared for regulatory change? Can governance evolve
over time? Can governance scale with AI capability growth? Are
future governance challenges understood? Is long-term AI oversight
sustainable? Is the AI governance environment future-ready?

About the OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™) is a growing
collection of structured questions designed to help identify,
understand, classify, and investigate problems before searching
for solutions.

Questions are organized into categories, clusters, and problem
environments to improve clarity, analysis, governance,
and decision-making.

The objective is not merely to provide answers.

The objective is to ensure that the right questions are being asked.
