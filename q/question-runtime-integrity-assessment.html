Modern systems do not remain static after deployment.

AI systems, autonomous agents, digital workflows, operational
platforms, and intelligent infrastructures continuously make
decisions, execute actions, interact with users, process exceptions,
and adapt to changing operational conditions.

Runtime Integrity Assessment exists to evaluate whether governance
integrity remains preserved during real-world operation.

This Question Index provides a structured collection of questions
designed to support runtime behavior evaluation, governance
integrity assessment, operational reality analysis, runtime evidence
review, drift detection, audit traceability, and long-term
governance improvement.

The objective is not to verify what the system was intended to do.

The objective is to verify what the system actually does.

About the OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™) is a growing collection of structured questions designed to help
identify, understand, classify, and investigate problems before searching for solutions.

Questions are organized into categories, clusters, and problem environments to improve clarity, analysis, governance,
and decision-making.

The objective is not merely to provide answers.

The objective is to ensure that the right questions are being asked.
