What Is Methodology Asset Formation™?

Methodology Asset Formation™ (MAF™) is an OOF® service that
transforms operational knowledge, industry expertise, innovative
ideas, governance opportunities, and market gaps into structured,
evaluated, documented, and protectable methodology assets.

Many valuable ideas never become usable methodologies because they
remain undocumented, unstructured, or lack a governance
architecture.

OOF® specializes in transforming knowledge into methodology.

Why This Service Exists

Organizations, researchers, startups, consultants, engineers, and
industry experts often recognize important operational problems or
market opportunities.

However, recognizing an opportunity is fundamentally different from
engineering a professional methodology.

Methodology development requires:

  • governance architecture,
  • methodology engineering,
  • operational analysis,
  • standards development,
  • canonical definitions,
  • architecture mapping,
  • documentation,
  • methodology protection,
  • long-term methodological consistency.


For many organizations and innovators, these capabilities are
difficult and expensive to build internally.

What OOF® Does

OOF® focuses on:

  • evaluating methodology potential,
  • identifying governance spaces,
  • performing Space Mapping™,
  • defining canonical meanings,
  • engineering governance architectures,
  • structuring standards and modules,
  • documenting methodologies,
  • strengthening methodological consistency,
  • positioning methodological intellectual property.


What OOF® Does Not Do by Default

Unless separately agreed:

  • OOF® does not raise investment capital.
  • OOF® does not guarantee investors.
  • OOF® does not perform commercial marketing.
  • OOF® does not operate the partner's business.
  • OOF® does not replace the founder, domain expert, or commercial owner.


The commercialization, fundraising, and operational deployment of
the methodology remain the responsibility of the methodology owner
or partner.
