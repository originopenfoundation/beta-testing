What Is Methodology Protection & Positioning™?

Methodology Protection & Positioning™ is a professional
service focused on helping organizations, founders,
innovators, and methodology creators protect, structure,
define, position, and strategically organize methodologies
as long-term intellectual and methodological assets.

Many methodologies contain valuable knowledge.

However, organizations often struggle with questions such as:
  • How should the methodology be named?
  • How should ownership be demonstrated?
  • How should the methodology be positioned?
  • How should the methodology be structured?
  • How should future expansion be supported?
  • How can methodology value be preserved?
Methodology Protection & Positioning™ exists to address
these challenges.

Why This Service Exists

Organizations routinely protect:

However, methodologies frequently remain:

Methodology Protection & Positioning™ was created to
