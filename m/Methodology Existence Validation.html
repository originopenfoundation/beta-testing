What Is Methodology Existence Validation™?

Methodology Existence Validation™ is a professional
service focused on documenting, preserving, validating,
and demonstrating the existence of methodologies through
structured evidence, historical records, authorship
documentation, version tracking, and methodology
origin records.

The service exists because valuable methodologies often
face a common challenge:

How can the existence of the methodology be demonstrated?

Organizations, founders, researchers, innovators, and
methodology creators frequently develop methodologies
long before commercialization, licensing, registration,
adoption, or public disclosure occurs.

Methodology Existence Validation™ helps establish structured
evidence supporting methodology existence and development history.

Why This Service Exists

Methodologies often evolve through:
  • ideas
  • drafts
  • notes
  • research
  • discussions
  • internal documents
  • frameworks
  • operational experience
Over time, organizations may need to demonstrate:
  • When did the methodology exist?
  • Who created it?
  • How did it evolve?
  • What evidence supports authorship?
  • What historical records exist?
  • What version history can be demonstrated?
  • How can methodology development be reconstructed?
Methodology Existence Validation™ was created to address
these challenges.
