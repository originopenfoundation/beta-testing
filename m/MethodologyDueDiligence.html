What Is Methodology Due Diligence™?

Methodology Due Diligence™ is a professional assessment service designed to
evaluate methodologies as strategic, operational, intellectual, and economic assets.

Organizations routinely perform:
  • Financial Due Diligence
  • Legal Due Diligence
  • Technical Due Diligence
  • Operational Due Diligence
However, one critical question often remains unanswered:

What is the value of the methodology itself?

Methodology Due Diligence™ was created to address this challenge.

Why This Service Exists

Many organizations depend on methodologies without fully understanding:
  • their strengths
  • their weaknesses
  • their risks
  • their scalability
  • their ownership status
  • their long-term value
Methodologies increasingly influence:
  • governance
  • artificial intelligence
  • operational systems
  • compliance programs
  • decision-making
  • organizational performance
Yet methodologies are rarely evaluated with the same rigor applied to
other strategic assets.

Methodology Due Diligence™ exists to provide that evaluation.

What We Assess

Our assessments are performed using the MVF™ — Methodology Assessment
& Valuation Framework™.

Depending on scope, assessments may include:
  • Methodology Asset Classification™
    What type of methodological asset exists?
  • Applicability Assessment™
    Can the methodology operate within real-world environments?
  • Adoption Assessment™
    Is the methodology being used and accepted?
  • Compatibility & Expansion Assessment™
    Can the methodology integrate, scale, and support future growth?
  • Operational Readiness Assessment™
    Is the methodology ready for deployment, governance, automation, and AI environments?
  • Protection & Ownership Assessment™
    Is the methodology protected, attributable, and defensible?
  • Proven Economic Value Assessment™
    What measurable value has already been created?
  • Strategic Value Assessment™
    What long-term strategic significance exists?
  • Future Potential & Risk Weighting Assessment™
    What opportunities and risks may influence future value?
