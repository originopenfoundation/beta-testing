Methodology Registry

OOF® — Methodology Authority™

Core Statement

Methodologies cannot be governed if they remain invisible.

They cannot be evaluated if they cannot be identified.

They cannot be reused if they cannot be discovered.

Methodology Registry™ exists to provide a structured system for
recording, organizing, classifying, and maintaining methodologies as
identifiable operational assets.

The objective is not ownership.

The objective is visibility.

Why a Registry Is Needed

Today, millions of methodologies exist across:

  • organizations,
  • universities,
  • governments,
  • consulting firms,
  • software companies,


research institutions.

Most remain:

  • undocumented,
  • fragmented,
  • inaccessible,
  • difficult to compare,


difficult to reuse.

As a result, the same methodological problems are repeatedly solved
from the beginning.

A registry creates visibility where fragmentation previously
existed.

What Is Methodology Registry™?

Methodology Registry™ is a structured framework for the
identification and organization of methodologies.

It provides:

  • registration,
  • classification,
  • discoverability,
  • traceability,


lifecycle visibility.

The registry does not determine whether a methodology is valid.

It records that the methodology exists.

Validation and evaluation occur separately.

Registry vs Validation

Registration is not validation.

Registration answers:

  • What exists?


Validation answers:

  • Does it work?


Compatibility answers:

  • Can it work with other methodologies?


Evaluation answers:

  • What are its impacts, risks, and limitations?


The registry serves as the foundational inventory layer beneath all
further assessment activities.

Core Registry Functions

Identification

Every methodology receives a unique identity within the registry.

This enables:

  • traceability,
  • referencing,
  • comparison,


future evolution tracking.

A methodology cannot be governed if it cannot be identified.

Classification

Methodologies are organized according to:

  • category,
  • domain,
  • operational space,
  • purpose,


architecture relationship.

Classification transforms collections into structured knowledge.

Discovery

Organizations must be able to discover methodologies relevant to
their needs.

Discovery enables:

  • reuse,
  • collaboration,
  • adaptation,


interoperability.

A methodology that cannot be found cannot be reused.

Traceability

Methodologies evolve.

The registry records:

  • origins,
  • revisions,
  • relationships,
  • dependencies,


derived methodologies.

This preserves methodological history.

Lifecycle Visibility

Methodologies may exist in different stages:

  • proposed,
  • registered,
  • evaluated,
  • compatible,
  • archived,


superseded.

The registry records methodological evolution over time.

Registry as Infrastructure

OOF® views methodology registries as infrastructure rather than
databases.

A database stores information.

A registry establishes reference points.

The difference is critical.

The purpose of Methodology Registry™ is not storage.

The purpose is methodological visibility and continuity.

Registry and Reusability

One of the largest inefficiencies in modern organizations is
repeated reinvention.

Teams frequently recreate:

  • workflows,
  • frameworks,
  • governance models,


operational procedures.

A methodology registry increases reuse by making existing
methodologies visible before new ones are created.

Reuse reduces waste.

Registry and Architecture Discovery

Methodology Registry™ supports the Architecture Discovery System™
(ADS™).

As methodologies accumulate, patterns emerge.

Repeated methodologies may indicate:

  • recurring problems,
  • recurring spaces,


recurring governance needs.

The registry therefore becomes a source of architectural
intelligence.

Relationship to Origin Open Registry™

Methodology Registry™ defines the registry concept.

Origin Open Registry™ (OOR™) serves as the implementation
environment within OOF®.

OOR™ will provide the operational infrastructure through which
methodologies can be recorded, explored, and connected.

Methodology Registry™ defines the framework.

Origin Open Registry™ implements it.

Why This Matters

Modern society has developed systems for registering:

  • companies,
  • patents,
  • trademarks,
  • domains,
  • products,


publications.

Yet methodologies—the structures that shape those systems—often
remain unregistered.

Methodology Registry™ addresses this gap.

It creates visibility for one of the most important yet least
visible layers of operational reality.

Final Statement

What is not visible cannot be reused.

What is not identified cannot be governed.

What is not registered cannot become part of a larger methodological
ecosystem.

Methodology Registry™ exists to make methodologies discoverable,
traceable, and governable across operational reality.
