What Is Methodology-as-a-Service™?

Methodology-as-a-Service™ (MaaS™) is a professional OOF® service
that enables organizations to obtain custom governance methodologies
through licensing instead of funding their complete internal
development.

Rather than investing significant time, resources, and expertise
into creating governance methodologies from scratch, organizations
can license professionally engineered methodologies designed
specifically for their operational environments.

MaaS™ transforms methodology development into a scalable,
continuously evolving governance service.

Why This Service Exists

Organizations continuously discover new operational challenges,
governance gaps, regulatory requirements, AI risks, accountability
issues, and business opportunities.

However, very few organizations possess dedicated methodology
engineering capabilities.

Developing professional governance methodologies requires:

  • governance architecture,
  • methodology engineering,
  • operational analysis,
  • standards development,
  • validation,
  • documentation,
  • continuous improvement,
  • long development cycles.


For many organizations, developing methodologies internally is
expensive, time-consuming, and difficult to maintain.

MaaS™ provides an alternative.

Organizations license governance methodologies instead of developing
them from scratch.
